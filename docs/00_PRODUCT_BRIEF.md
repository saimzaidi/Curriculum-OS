# Product Brief

## Product name

**CurriculumOS**

## One-line description

An adaptive teaching system that turns school source material into a living curriculum and recompiles future teaching when classroom reality changes.

## Problem

Teachers and schools create course outlines, lesson plans, notes, activities, homework, quizzes, revision packs, and progress reports separately.

These artifacts drift apart because:

- the calendar changes;
- classes are cancelled;
- some concepts take longer than expected;
- students fail prerequisite concepts;
- different sections move at different speeds;
- generated material is not linked to what was actually taught;
- existing AI tools produce isolated documents rather than maintaining a coherent teaching system.

## Solution

CurriculumOS creates a connected curriculum model linking:

- source pages;
- concepts;
- prerequisites;
- learning objectives;
- calendar sessions;
- lessons;
- activities;
- homework;
- assessments;
- student mastery;
- revision;
- teacher edits.

When classroom conditions change, CurriculumOS recomputes only what must change and preserves teacher locks and exam-critical content.

## Primary users

### Teacher

Needs planning, generation, progress tracking, remediation, and control.

### Academic coordinator

Needs planned-versus-actual coverage, section consistency, risk visibility, and curriculum quality.

### School or academy owner

Needs standardized teaching packages, lower preparation cost, and repeatable delivery.

## Core differentiator

Most AI education products generate content.

CurriculumOS maintains a source-grounded, versioned curriculum that continuously adapts planning, assessment, and teaching material to actual classroom progress.

## Core modules

- **Core** — source ingestion and curriculum graph.
- **Pace** — deterministic scheduling and replanning.
- **Recall** — mastery analysis and remediation.
- **Lens** — localization, reading level, and differentiation.
- **Forge** — lesson and assessment generation.
- **Ledger** — coverage, history, and administrative visibility.
- **Pack** — offline HTML and print export.

## Product principles

1. Teacher judgment outranks AI output.
2. Every generated item must be traceable to sources.
3. Schedules must be deterministic and explainable.
4. The product must work under weak connectivity.
5. Localization means rewriting for local comprehension, not literal translation.
6. The system must distinguish required concepts from optional enrichment.
7. Replanning should minimize unnecessary disruption.
